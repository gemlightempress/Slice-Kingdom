Privacy Policy

Slice Kingdom

Overview

Slice Kingdom is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Locally stored game data: progress, Golden Seeds and Royal Grapes balances, XP, unlocked Boards/Swords/Boosters, and settings are stored on your device and are not transmitted to our servers by the base game.
  • Google Play Games sign-in (required): a Google Play Games account is required to play. When you sign in, Google Play Games Services accesses your Google display name, avatar, and player identifier, and stores your Achievements and Leaderboard scores on Google's servers, governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Player Profile data: your Player Profile (level, stats, mode records, and a locally generated Player ID) is stored on your device and backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save — see "Cloud Save & Progress Sync" below.
  • Advertising identifiers: if advertising is enabled (Google AdMob), the ad network may collect device advertising identifiers, coarse location, and interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's ad-tracking settings.
  • Purchase data: in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not directly receive or store your payment card details.
  • Diagnostic data: anonymous crash and performance data may be collected to improve stability.

How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress locally, and back it up to Google Play Games Cloud Save (see "Cloud Save & Progress Sync" below).
  • To display your Achievements and Leaderboard standing through Google Play Games Services.
  • To display and measure advertising (where applicable) through Google AdMob.
  • To process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve game performance.

Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Games Services (required sign-in, Achievements, Leaderboards)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (purchases)
  • Google Play Services (distribution, crash reporting)

Children's Privacy

The Game is designed to be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for functionality such as advertising, which is configured to respect applicable child-directed treatment settings (e.g., Google's Families Ad Program) when the app is listed for a young audience.

Cloud Save & Progress Sync

Your progress, Golden Seeds and Royal Grapes balances, XP, unlocked Boards/Swords/Boosters, and settings are saved on your device and automatically backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save while you are signed in. Signing in with the same Google Play account on another device restores this progress there. If the same account is used on two devices, the most recently updated save is kept as the active one; the older save is not merged with it. A manual "Sync Now" option is also available in Player Profile. If you play without an internet connection, your progress continues saving locally and backs up automatically the next time you're online.

Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored progress can be erased at any time by clearing app data/storage through your device settings. Cloud-saved progress, Achievements, and Leaderboard data associated with your Google Play account are retained and deleted according to your Google Account settings.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.
  • You can disable vibration, sound, and music independently in the in-game Settings screen.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to the Game or applicable law.
