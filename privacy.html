Privacy Policy

What Slice Kingdom collects, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Slice Kingdom is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Game Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain game-related information on your device, including:

  • Your Golden Seeds and Royal Grapes
  • Boosters
  • Unlocked boards, swords and themes
  • Level progress and best scores
  • Achievements
  • Daily reward streak
  • Daily Challenge progress
  • The display name you choose
  • If you are signed in to Google Play Games: a copy of your Play Games display name and profile picture, used for your in-game profile
  • Whether you have completed (or skipped) the first-time tutorial
  • Sound, music, vibration and reminder settings

Google Play Games Sign-In

The game uses Google Play Games Services. Signing in is optional, and the game is fully playable without it.

  • If you have already signed in to this game with Google Play Games, Google signs you in automatically in the background when the game starts. Google may show its own brief “Welcome” banner.
  • If you have not signed in yet, the game simply continues as a guest and does not open any sign-in screen on its own. Google’s own Play Games sign-in screen appears only if you choose to sign in yourself.
  • You can sign in at any time by tapping “Sign in with Google Play” in Settings → Player Profile.

If you sign in, Google Play Games Services provides your:

  • Player ID
  • Display name
  • Profile picture

Your display name and profile picture appear on your profile in Settings, and a copy is kept on your device. They are not sent to any server of ours. Signing in also allows your game progress to be backed up using Google Play Games Cloud Save.

Purchases

In-app purchases of Royal Grapes are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google. We receive only confirmation of what was purchased.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows advertisements through Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads in a reserved strip at the bottom of the screen. The strip appears on every screen except the home screen and while you are playing, and the screens are sized to sit above it so it never covers the game.
  • Full-screen ads between games, for example after you finish a game and tap Play Again or Home.
  • Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus: continuing a run with an extra life when your lives run out (Revive), or opening a chest.

Google AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data to serve and measure advertisements, subject to your device’s advertising settings.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before ads load, asking whether ads may be personalised. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device’s own advertising settings, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used, so we can improve it and fix problems. It records usage events such as app opens, screens viewed, session length and advertisements shown or rewarded, together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version and approximate country. It does not record your name or email address.

The game also uses Firebase Crashlytics, which records a report when the game crashes or hits an error: the device model, operating system version, app version and a technical stack trace. These reports contain no personal details and are used only to find and fix faults.

App Updates

When the game starts it asks Google Play whether a newer version of the app is available, so it can offer you the update. This check is handled entirely by Google Play and sends us nothing about you.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Save your game progress and settings on your device.
  • Back up and sync your progress through Google Play Games Cloud Save while you are signed in.
  • Show your name and profile picture (your chosen name, or your Google Play Games name and picture if you sign in) on your in-game profile.
  • Deliver Royal Grapes that you purchase.
  • Display and measure advertising through Google AdMob, and ask for your consent where required.
  • Understand how the game is played and improve it, using Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Send reminders, such as when your daily reward and a new Daily Challenge are ready. Reminders are on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings; they are scheduled entirely on your device, and Android asks for notification permission before any is shown.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, all provided by Google. Each is governed by Google’s own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in, player profile, and Cloud Save.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, including Google’s consent form where required.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — gameplay analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting.
  • Google Play Core (In-App Updates) — checking whether a newer version is available.

Children’s Privacy

The game is designed to be suitable for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for the game to function, such as advertising, which is configured to respect applicable child-directed treatment settings when the app is listed for a young audience.

Cloud Save & Progress Sync

While you are signed in, your progress is saved on your device and backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save automatically.

Playing on another device with the same Google account can restore your progress there. When the progress on a device and in your Cloud Save has both changed, the game shows you both and asks which one to keep.

If you play offline, your progress continues to be saved on your device and will sync the next time you are online. You can also manually initiate synchronization by tapping “Sync Now” in Settings → Player Profile.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can erase your game progress at any time by using “Reset Progress” in Settings.

This clears the progress stored on your device and, if you are signed in, replaces your Cloud Save with a fresh start.

You can also clear the game’s data through your device settings or uninstall the game.

Any data held by Google in connection with your Google account is retained and deleted according to your Google Account settings.

Your Choices

You have the following choices:

  • You can play entirely without signing in — the game never requires an account or an internet connection. You can sign in later with “Sign in with Google Play” in Settings → Player Profile.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising, and reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device’s advertising settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device’s advertising settings.
  • You can turn off sound, music, vibration, and reminders in Settings.
  • You can change your display name at any time from your profile in Settings.
  • You can erase your game progress at any time using “Reset Progress” in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game or applicable law.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date above and ask you to review the updated policy in the game.
