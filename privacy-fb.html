Privacy Policy

What Slice Kingdom collects, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Slice Kingdom is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Game Data Saved With Your Facebook Account

The game runs on Facebook Instant Games. Your progress is saved with Facebook, using the game-data storage that Facebook provides, so it follows you across the devices where you play. It includes:

  • Your Golden Seeds and Royal Grapes
  • Boosters
  • Unlocked boards, swords and themes
  • Level progress and best scores
  • Achievements and their claimed state
  • Daily reward streak
  • Daily Challenge progress
  • Whether you have completed (or skipped) the first-time tutorial
  • Sound, music and vibration settings
  • A record of which purchases have already been delivered, so that nothing is delivered twice

The game does not ask for, receive or store your name, profile picture, friends list or email address. Facebook identifies you to the game only by an anonymous player ID that is used to keep your save. This data is stored by Facebook; it is not sent to any server of ours.

Purchases

In-app purchases of Royal Grapes are processed through Facebook Payments. Payment details are handled entirely by Facebook and its payment partners. We receive only confirmation of what was purchased.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows advertisements provided by Meta (Meta Audience Network) through Facebook Instant Games:

  • Banner ads in a strip at the bottom of the screen on menu screens (on phones).
  • Full-screen ads between games, for example after you finish a game and tap Play Again or Home.
  • Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus: continuing a run with an extra life when your lives run out (Revive), or opening a chest.

Advertisements are selected and measured by Meta, which may use information about you according to your Facebook ad preferences and settings and Meta’s own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from these ads. You can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook settings.

Sharing and Inviting Friends

If you choose Share in Settings, the game opens Facebook’s own Invite Friends or Share Game dialog. The game sends only a picture and the text “Come play Slice with me!”; you choose who or where to send it, inside Facebook’s dialog. The game does not receive your name, profile picture or friends list, and nothing is given or unlocked for sharing or inviting.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Save your game progress and settings with Facebook, so you can continue on any device where you play.
  • Deliver Royal Grapes that you purchase.
  • Display advertising through Meta, and let you choose to watch rewarded ads for bonuses.

Third-Party Services

The game is hosted and run by Facebook Instant Games and uses these Meta services. They are governed by Meta’s privacy policy:

  • Facebook Instant Games — running the game and saving your progress.
  • Meta Audience Network — advertising.
  • Facebook Payments — in-app purchases.

The game itself does not use any analytics, crash-reporting or advertising-identifier service of its own, and it sends no notifications.

Children’s Privacy

The game is designed to be suitable for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for the game to function, such as advertising, which is configured to respect applicable child-directed treatment settings when the app is listed for a young audience.

Progress Sync

Your progress is saved with your Facebook account automatically and is available on any device where you open the game. If you go offline, the game keeps running and saves again when you are back online.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can erase your game progress at any time by using “Reset Progress” in Settings. This replaces your saved progress with a fresh start.

Data held by Facebook in connection with your Facebook account is retained and deleted according to Facebook’s own policies and your Facebook settings.

Your Choices

  • You can turn off sound, music and vibration in Settings.
  • You can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook settings.
  • You can erase your game progress at any time using “Reset Progress” in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game or applicable law.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date above and ask you to review the updated policy in the game.
